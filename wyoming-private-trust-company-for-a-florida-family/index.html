A Wyoming Private Trust Company for a Florida Family

By Rafael Recalde — November 22, 2026

Recalde Law Firm, P.A. is a Florida law firm with an office in Miami, Florida. We advise on United States law. What follows is general. It describes no family's facts and no family's numbers. Tax results are confirmed with the family's tax adviser. We advise on the legal structure.

A Florida family that wants a trustee other than a sibling, and other than a bank, sometimes looks at a private family trust company. Wyoming law allows that company to be a limited liability company. The managers can be the people the family already trusts. Those people can live in Miami. Nobody in the family has to take up residence in Wyoming. The company is the trustee. An outside trust company is optional.

The statute, the waiver delivered to the banking commissioner, the line between an unsupervised company and a chartered one, and the definition of who counts as family are already covered in Wyoming private trust companies. The practice page is private trust companies. This note does not restate them. Formation and the question of who fits the statutory family are Wyoming counsel's work. We coordinate. We do not practice Wyoming banking law.

What the family is actually taking on

The company needs managers, a succession when a manager dies or resigns, and a record of decisions. The trust instrument has to name the company as trustee and match the powers the managers will exercise. A company that exists as a name, with no one who will sign an account form or approve a distribution, is an individual-trustee problem with an extra filing. The reason to use the company is that the family will run it.

The managers' residence in Florida does not decide the trust's governing law. A Wyoming trust still needs the Wyoming connection the Wyoming statutes describe, which is the subject of a Wyoming irrevocable trust when you live in Florida. The company can be that connection when it administers as the statute contemplates. The family stays in Florida.

How the roles split

The company is often the administrative trustee: records, custody, tax filings, and carrying out directions. Investment decisions and distribution decisions can sit with advisers under a directed-trust instrument. Those roles, and the powers the grantor should not hold, are in directed trusts. Putting every power in the managers, including the power to distribute to the people who appointed them, is a design the tax adviser reads under sections 2036 and 2038 before the company is formed.

For a domestic trust with U.S. persons making the decisions, the foreign-trust control test discussed in the existing Wyoming note is usually the wrong frame. That note remains the right one when the question is whether a trust is foreign. Classification of the LLC itself is a tax question for the family's tax adviser. The legal structure is the operating agreement, the trust, and the account the custodian will open for the company as trustee.

Write as the family, or as the adviser.
