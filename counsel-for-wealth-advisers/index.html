Counsel for Wealth Advisers

Outside counsel for wealth advisers on the U.S. legal architecture of a foreign trust.

You keep the client relationship. We design or repair the U.S. side of the structure when a family you advise has a foreign trust, U.S. assets, or a beneficiary who is or may become a U.S. person. The family is the client of the engagement. You remain their banker, planner, family office, or RIA.

What we take, and what we do not

This is not Advisers Act counsel, and it is not a request that you hand over the family. Investment advice, custody, and the client's planning relationship stay with you. Tax computations and the returns stay with the family's tax adviser. We are U.S. counsel for the legal structure.

FGT and FNGT classification

We read the deed against sections 671 through 679, section 672(f), and the court and control tests in section 7701. The work product is a classification the tax adviser can file from, and amendments when the powers on paper are not the powers the family thinks it has. See FGT vs. FNGT and Foreign Trusts.

Form 3520, as a legal question

We identify who has a duty under section 6048, whether a distribution is a gift or a trust distribution, and whether a U.S. owner exists so that Form 3520-A is even the right return. Your client's CPA prepares the form. See Form 3520 and Form 3520-A.

Estate tax on U.S.-situs assets

Real estate in Florida and stock of U.S. corporations can sit inside a foreign trust and still be in a non-resident alien's U.S. gross estate. We separate that estate-tax question from the income-tax classification of the trust, and we coordinate the holding structure with the exemption of $60,000. See U.S.-situs assets inside a foreign trust and Wealth structuring for foreign nationals.

When a beneficiary becomes a U.S. person

A green card, substantial presence, or a U.S.-born child changes the stack you have been administering. Section 679, PFIC and CFC exposure, and Form 3520 can turn on before anyone revises the investment policy statement. We do that review with you, before the status change if there is still time. See When a family member becomes a U.S. person.

How an engagement runs

You introduce the family, or the family writes and names you. We run a conflict check before anyone sends the deed or account statements. The scope letter states the legal work and names what it is not: not investment advice, not the tax opinion, not RIA compliance. Calls are joint when you want to be on them. Tax counsel is on the calls that decide a filing position. You receive what the family authorizes you to receive.

Family offices and referring lawyers

A multi-generational stack — trust, holding companies, and accounts a bank will actually open — is described on Foreign trusts for family offices. A lawyer who wants to refer or stay in as co-counsel should use Refer a cross-border matter. The broader private-wealth practice, beyond the trust, is Private Wealth.
