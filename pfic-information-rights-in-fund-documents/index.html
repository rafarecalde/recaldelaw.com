Fund Formation & Cross-Border Structuring

Information Rights as a Structural Condition, Not a Courtesy

By Rafael Recalde — November 12, 2026

In this series. The annual information statement is signed by the PFIC or a person authorized to sign for it. A manager who controls the lower tier can put that duty in the documents. A manager who holds a minority of someone else's fund cannot. The position that results is the holding you cannot elect over. The practice page is Cayman funds.

A manager who controls the lower-tier funds can cause them to produce what a qualified electing fund election requires. A manager who holds a minority position in someone else's fund cannot compel it. The analysis is not whether the other sponsor is cooperative. The analysis is who is authorized to sign. Information undertakings belong in the documents before the offering goes out.

The signature is the PFIC's

Treasury Regulation section 1.1295-1(g)(1) describes the PFIC Annual Information Statement as a statement of the PFIC, signed by the PFIC or an authorized representative of the PFIC. The contents are the taxable year, the shareholder's pro rata shares of ordinary earnings and net capital gain or information sufficient to calculate them or access to the books to calculate them under federal income tax principles, the cash and the fair market value of other property distributed or deemed distributed, and the undertaking to permit inspection of the books that support the computation. The alternative to the inspection undertaking is a private letter ruling and a closing agreement, which paragraph (g)(2) confines to rare and unusual circumstances.

Authority to sign for the PFIC is a question of that entity's documents and of the law under which it is organized. Counsel in that jurisdiction reads the authority. We read whether the U.S. undertaking is in a document that binds the person who will have to perform it. A data room, an administrator's file, and a willingness to be helpful can be how numbers are gathered. They are not the representation paragraph (g) requires, and they are not a signature.

Control is the ability to bind the signatory

If the sponsor directs the lower-tier vehicle, the documents of that vehicle can contain the undertaking before anyone subscribes. The vehicle agrees to compute ordinary earnings and net capital gain under U.S. income tax principles, or to open the books for that computation; to report cash and property distributed; to permit inspection of the records that support the computation; and to have an authorized person sign the statement. That is a condition of launching a vehicle whose U.S. holders may need to elect. It is documentary work. It is not a promise that the computation will come out in any particular way.

If the sponsor is a minority holder of a third-party fund, no clause in the sponsor's own offering creates authority inside the third-party fund. A request can be made. A document the other sponsor did not sign cannot compel the statement. The analysis ends at authority. The cash sleeve that sits in that kind of fund is a classification problem with an operational response, which is to hold the instruments in a vehicle the manager does control, as a policy adopted before the purchase.

An intermediary statement does not fill a missing signature. Section 1.1295-1(g)(3) allows the intermediary statement only if the intermediary received the PFIC's statement, or a chain of intermediary statements leading to it. There is nothing to pass along if the PFIC never issued the statement. A combined statement under paragraph (g)(4) still has to identify each corporation separately, and it is still the PFIC's information.

Before the offering, not after the first closing

The undertaking belongs in the documents that will be circulated. A side arrangement after the first closing arrives after positions may already be on the books. The election year is the year of acquisition. A covenant negotiated the following spring does not move that year.

Where the lower tiers are partnerships, the number of PFIC elections falls and the need for their numbers does not. The upper fund's statement is built from what those vehicles report. If the sponsor controls them, the duty to deliver the figures that the upper computation requires belongs in their documents as well. Partnership classification is a separate decision, with its own limits.

This is U.S. documentary coordination. It is not a representation that a given manager can obtain a statement from a fund the manager does not control, and it is not a representation that an election will be available. The shareholder makes the election. The fund, if it is bound and if it is the PFIC, provides the statement.
