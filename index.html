Private Wealth

Wealthy families and business owners in the United States and Latin America. The family is the client.

+

The family is the client. For a wealthy U.S. family or a business owner, the work is the structure around a business, a sale or liquidity event, a holding company, and children. For an international family, it is the U.S. structure when assets and family members sit in more than one country: foreign grantor and non-grantor trusts, holding companies, classification elections, and private placement life insurance. Family offices, private banks, and RIAs keep the relationships they already have. We advise the family and coordinate with them.

We coordinate with the family's CPA on the tax result of each approach. Investment advice stays with the wealth adviser. Advisers Act and RIA-compliance counsel is a separate engagement, for the adviser firm. Where the question is how a person who is neither a U.S. citizen nor domiciled in the United States holds U.S. assets, the federal estate-tax exemption for those U.S.-situs assets is generally $60,000, unless an estate tax treaty allows more. That narrower engagement is on U.S. holdings for non-U.S. persons.

Representative Matters

  • Structures for a wealthy U.S. family around a business sale or liquidity event, including GRATs, sales to grantor trusts, dynasty trusts, and spousal lifetime access trusts
  • Family holding companies and LLCs for a closely held business
  • Foreign grantor and non-grantor trust design, including Forms 3520 and 3520-A coordination
  • Transfers of closely held equity before a liquidity event or rollover, coordinated with deal counsel
  • Private placement life insurance as part of the holding stack, with product design left to the carrier and the family's insurance advisers
  • Form 8832 classification elections for foreign holding structures and cross-border partnerships
  • U.S. estate tax on real property, brokerage accounts, and other U.S.-situs assets of a person who is neither a U.S. citizen nor domiciled in the United States, where the federal exemption is generally $60,000 unless an estate tax treaty allows more
  • Restructuring when someone already in the family is or becomes a U.S. person, including PFIC exposure
  • Structuring funds and SPVs, and advising family offices on the family's direct private equity investments — co-investments, and the subscription and investor documents behind them

Fund Formation & RIA Counsel

Structuring private funds and advising registered and exempt reporting advisers from formation through ongoing operations.

+

We work with emerging and established managers to structure, document, and launch private investment vehicles — including venture capital funds, private equity funds, hedge funds, and real asset vehicles. Our fund formation work covers the full lifecycle: entity structuring, drafting of limited partnership agreements and operating agreements, private placement memoranda, subscription documents, and side letter negotiations.

For investment advisers, we serve as ongoing outside regulatory counsel. We advise SEC-registered investment advisers and exempt reporting advisers on the Advisers Act compliance framework, including Form ADV preparation and amendments, compliance program design and annual reviews, code of ethics, custody rule obligations, advertising and marketing rule compliance under the new Marketing Rule, and books and records requirements.

Representative Matters

  • Formation of Delaware limited partnerships and LLCs for multi-strategy fund platforms
  • Drafting and negotiation of LPAs, PPMs, and subscription agreements
  • SEC and state investment adviser registration and Form ADV preparation
  • Exempt reporting adviser filings and ongoing compliance
  • Compliance program design, mock examinations, and annual reviews
  • Side letter negotiations and MFN provision management
  • Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1) compliance and advertisement review
  • Custody rule analysis and surprise examination coordination
  • CCO support and outsourced compliance consulting

Mergers & Acquisitions

Business owners, buyers, sellers, and investors in a sale or acquisition, from the letter of intent through closing.

+

We represent principals on both sides of middle-market acquisitions, dispositions, recapitalizations, and joint ventures. Our approach is hands-on and deal-oriented — we quarterback transactions from the letter of intent through closing and post-closing adjustments, coordinating with tax advisors, accountants, and lenders to keep deals on track.

We have particular experience with transactions involving regulated entities — including acquisitions of RIA practices, fund portfolio transfers, and transactions where securities law intersects with traditional M&A. We also advise on cross-border M&A where U.S. and Latin American parties are on opposite sides of the table.

Representative Matters

  • Buy-side and sell-side representation in asset and stock acquisitions
  • Due diligence management and coordination across work streams
  • Drafting and negotiation of purchase agreements, earn-outs, and escrow arrangements
  • Acquisitions and sales of registered investment advisory practices
  • Joint venture structuring and operating agreement negotiation
  • Management buyouts and recapitalizations
  • Post-closing purchase price adjustment disputes
  • Cross-border transaction structuring with LATAM counterparties

Corporate & Private Offerings

Private offerings and equity arrangements for business owners and companies.

+

We counsel business owners and companies raising capital in a private offering: which exemption fits, how the offering is structured, who may invest, and what the disclosure has to say. The work includes Regulation D offerings and Regulation S offerings to non-U.S. persons.

The same work covers equity compensation — stock option plans, restricted stock, profits interests, and phantom equity — and the documents for a priced round, a SAFE or convertible note conversion, and a secondary sale. The regulatory requirements and the cap table are read together.

Representative Matters

  • Regulation D (Rule 506(b) and 506(c)) private placement structuring and documentation
  • Regulation S offshore offering compliance for non-U.S. investor tranches
  • SAFE, convertible note, and priced equity round documentation
  • Private placement memoranda, term sheets, and subscription agreements
  • Stock option plans, restricted stock, profits interests, and phantom equity design
  • Equity compensation for owners, executives, and key employees
  • Accredited investor verification and bad actor disqualification analysis
  • Form D filings and state blue sky notice filings
  • Secondary transfers and Rule 144 compliance

Commercial Litigation & International Arbitration

Representing businesses and investors in international arbitration and complex commercial disputes in U.S. federal and state courts.

+

We represent businesses, investors, and funds in international commercial arbitration proceedings under ICC, AAA/ICDR, and JAMS rules, as well as in ad hoc proceedings governed by agreement. Our arbitration work spans cross-border contract disputes, investment-related claims, shareholder and joint venture disagreements, and post-closing M&A disputes between parties in different jurisdictions. Miami's position as a hub for U.S.-Latin America commerce means many of our clients face disputes that cross borders — and require counsel fluent in both the procedural demands of international arbitration and the substantive law of multiple legal systems.

We also litigate complex commercial disputes in Florida state and federal courts, including before the Southern District of Florida and the Eleventh Circuit. Our litigation practice concentrates in areas where our transactional and regulatory background provides a tactical edge — securities fraud, investment adviser disputes, partnership and LLC controversies, and failed transactions. We handle matters from pre-litigation demand strategy through trial, appeal, and enforcement of judgments and arbitral awards.

Who We Serve

For private wealth, the family is the client. Family offices, private banks, and RIAs work alongside us. Fund sponsors come for formation. Business owners come for a sale or a financing.

Wealthy U.S. Families

The family is the client. Business owners and families after a liquidity event, including the holding company, the sale, and what moves to children. We coordinate with the family's CPA, wealth adviser, and trust company.

International Families

The family is the client. We advise on the U.S. legal structure when family members or assets sit in more than one country, and we coordinate with the family offices, private banks, and RIAs who already work with them. A co-investment or an SPV is documented for the family. Investment-adviser registration is a separate matter, for the adviser who needs it.

Latin American Families

Families and business owners in Latin America who are buying U.S. real property, a business, or financial assets. We advise the family on the U.S. legal structure, including pre-immigration planning and FIRPTA, and we coordinate with the family's home-country advisers and with the U.S. tax adviser.

Business Owners

Owners of a closely held business, including a sale, a recapitalization, or a private offering. The work covers the purchase agreement or the offering documents, the holding company, and the equity that stays with the owner or moves to the family.

Fund Sponsors

General partners and sponsors forming and operating private funds across venture capital, private equity, credit, and real asset strategies. We handle fund formation, investor onboarding, regulatory filings, and the ongoing governance and compliance needs of fund operations.

Investment Advisers

When the client is the adviser firm itself — a registered adviser or an exempt reporting adviser — the work is formation, Form ADV, and the compliance program. That is separate from private wealth, where the family is the client and the adviser works alongside us.

About Rafael Recalde

All client matters are led by Rafael Recalde, the firm's managing attorney.

He is licensed in Florida and Washington, D.C., and brings over 18 years of experience representing companies, investors, and business owners in transactional, litigation, and regulatory matters. His practice spans private wealth, fund formation, M&A, and complex business disputes, with particular work for families in the United States and Latin America.

Earlier in his career, Rafael held legal clerkships at Citigroup's Latin America Legal Department, where he supported the General Counsel in coordinating legal teams — both in-house and external — across 24 jurisdictions in the region. He also gained regulatory experience at the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

Fluent in English and Spanish, Rafael also speaks working French, Italian, and Portuguese, which strengthens his ability to engage with international stakeholders, review foreign-language documents, and streamline cross-border coordination.

Rafael is admitted to the Florida Bar and the District of Columbia Bar, and is authorized to practice before the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit.

Based in Miami, Florida.
