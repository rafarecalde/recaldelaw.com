## Why these ads are not tombstone ads

Rule 4-7.19(a) requires a lawyer to file each non-exempt advertisement with The Florida Bar at least 20 days before it is first disseminated.

Rule 4-7.20(a) exempts an ad that contains no illustrations and no information other than the presumptively valid “tombstone” content in Rule 4-7.16. The Florida Bar’s public summary of that list, for a lawyer or law firm, is: firm and lawyer names, office location, phone, website, email, a designation such as “attorney” or “law firm,” fields of law in which the lawyer actually practices, bar membership and dates, education, languages, and a short list of other factual items (including a fee schedule, which this draft does not use). A call to action, a description of what a trust does, a mention of children, probate, homestead, or a named trust product is outside that list.

Rule 4-7.20(g) exempts the lawyer’s own website from the filing requirement, including this landing page. The page still has to comply with the substantive advertising rules (Rules 4-7.12 through 4-7.17). A single page may be filed voluntarily under Rule 4-7.19(d). The Bar does not accept a filing of an entire website.

Internet search ads are not the website. They are public-media advertisements. Each responsive search ad below mixes tombstone lines with other lines, and Google can assemble those lines into one impression. **All three ads should be filed under Rule 4-7.19** at least 20 days before they run, unless they are cut down to the tombstone-only set at the end of this file. The filing fee is the amount Rule 4-7.19(h) currently sets. Confirm the current fee with the Bar. A later wording change is a new advertisement.

Rule 4-7.12 requires every ad to include the name of the lawyer or law firm and the city, town, or county of a bona fide office of the lawyer who will perform the services. In each ad below, pin **Recalde Law Firm** to headline position 1 and **Miami, Florida** to headline position 2 so those two lines are present in every combination. An unpinned ad can serve a combination that omits one of them.

Rule 4-7.14: do not say specialist, expert, certified, or best. None of these lines do. “Trusts and Estate Planning” and “Estate Planning” are offered as fields of practice under Rule 4-7.16, which is accurate only if the firm in fact practices in that field.

Do not put the $15 million exclusion or the $19,000 annual exclusion in an ad. A dollar figure in a 90-character line drops the qualifiers (per person, 2026, indexed, present interest, tax adviser). Those figures stay on the landing page.

## Negative keywords

Add these at the campaign level. Prefer phrase match for multi-word negatives and broad match only for the single junk words listed under “single-word negatives,” so a word inside another word (for example “form” inside “formation”) is not blocked.

### Single-word negatives

free, diy, template, templates, cheap, cheapest, affordable, inexpensive, jobs, job, career, salary, hiring, internship, school, schools, college, university, course, courses, class, classes, degree, exam, quizlet, wikipedia, sample, samples, pdf, download, kit, software, forms

“form” as its own negative blocks queries that contain the word “form” (revocable trust form, trust form Florida). It does not block “formation.”
