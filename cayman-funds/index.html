Scope

What we lead, and what stays with Cayman counsel.

The useful division is simple. U.S. documents and U.S. regulatory analysis are our work. Cayman law, the Cayman entity, and the CIMA filing are Cayman counsel’s work. The two have to describe the same fund.

U.S. work we lead

  • Structure: which U.S. and Cayman vehicles hold the manager, the general partner or sponsor, and the investors, including feeder and parallel arrangements
  • U.S. fund documents: partnership or LLC agreements on the U.S. side, management agreements, private placement materials, subscriptions, and investor questionnaires
  • Offering exemptions, Investment Company Act exclusions, and the adviser’s U.S. registration or exemption position
  • Coordination with your tax advisers on classification, reporting, and information the investors will need — without a promise about the tax result

Cayman work for Cayman counsel

  • Cayman legal advice on the vehicle and on whether the Mutual Funds Act or the Private Funds Act applies
  • Formation of the Cayman company, partnership, or other vehicle
  • CIMA registration and the Cayman offering and constitutional documents
  • Cayman governance and licensing questions, including any securities-investment-business analysis. An exemption is not automatic

The decisions

Four questions worth answering before the first subscription.

Each of these is a separate note. None of them is a substitute for advice on a particular fund.

Exit rights Open-ended or closed-ended

Can an investor require redemption, or is capital committed until distributions and realizations? The statute usually follows that answer. The entity type does not decide it.

 Where the money goes Feeder or parallel

A feeder invests into another fund. Parallel funds invest alongside each other. International investors do not, by themselves, require either.

 Who does the work Board, GP, manager, administrator

The operator follows the legal form. The investment manager and the administrator are different seats. A manager outside Cayman is not an exemption.

 When cash moves Lock-ups, gates, and payment

A dealing date, a valuation date, and a payment date are three dates. Open-ended does not mean cash on demand, and a feeder cannot pay before it is paid.

How a matter usually proceeds

The order of the work, not a promised timetable.

Every fund moves at the speed of its documents, its service providers, and its regulators. What follows is the sequence we use. It is not a completion date.

1

Exit rights and the assets

We start with what investors can require, and whether the portfolio can support that timetable. Registration does not create the cash.

2

One vehicle, a feeder, or a parallel pair

We map where each investment sits and where each fee is charged, including affiliate arrangements and offsets.

3

U.S. documents and U.S. regulatory analysis

Offering exemptions, the Investment Company Act, the adviser’s position, and coordination with your tax advisers on the information investors will need.

4

Cayman counsel on the Cayman vehicle

Formation, Cayman legal advice, governance appointments, and CIMA registration stay with qualified Cayman counsel. We coordinate. We do not replace them.
